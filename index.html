Score timelines: changepoints, and driver term word clouds

Blue line is the raw monthly score (including all languages).
Dashed markers are changepoints, hover over a changepoint to see the driver terms word cloud.
Faint grey markers are invalid changepoints, this is usually due to it not having enough english reviews to go through analysis.
Driver clouds are English review text only, which is why some changepoints have no cloud.
Word size is uses within the three months after the changepoint, counting at most five uses per review so one copy-pasted review cannot dominate, so sizes compare inside one cloud but not between games.
Size sets height, and a longer term is wider at the same height, so it covers more of the cloud than a short term used as often. Hover the terms for exact counts.
Color is the share of reviews using that word that were thumbs-up, running from green (100% thumbs up) to red (0% thumb up/100% thumbs down).
